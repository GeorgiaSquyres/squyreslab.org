# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-user-plus" %}Join us

We are excited to work with scientists at all levels and from all backgrounds who are curious about the microbial world, who are open to new and interdisciplinary methods, and who love bacteria, emergent properties, or both. We combine rigorous science with fun and exploration, and like bacteria in a biofilm, we tackle hard problems by working together.

As we get started, there will be several opportunities to join our group. Please stay tuned!  

***Lab officer:*** We are hiring a Laboratory Officer, a unique role that combines lab management, technical development, and independent research. [See the job ad and apply here.](https://embl.wd103.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/EMBL/job/Heidelberg/Laboratory-Officer---Squyres-Group_JR4797)

***PhD applicants:*** Please apply through the [EMBL International PhD Programme.](https://www.embl.org/about/info/embl-international-phd-programme/) Applications are opened twice a year, once in summer and once in winter. Please see the program website for application instructions and relevant deadlines.

***Postdoctoral applicants:*** Please reach out to Georgia by e-mail to introduce yourself and share your interests for your postdoc, and include your CV. Our group also often participates in EMBL's [Postdoc Fellowship Programmes.](https://www.embl.org/about/info/postdoctoral-programme/fellowship-programmes/)

***Master's students, undergraduate interns, and visitors:*** Our group is open to interns and visitors. Please reach out to Georgia by e-mail to introduce yourself, and include your research interests, CV, and time frame. Student visitors are also welcomed through the [Erasmus+ program.](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/)
