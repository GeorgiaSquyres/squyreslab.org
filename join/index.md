# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-user-plus" %}Join us

We are excited to work with scientists at all levels and from all backgrounds who are curious about the microbial world, who are open to new and interdisciplinary methods, and who love bacteria, emergent properties, or both. We combine rigorous science with fun and exploration, and like bacteria in a biofilm, we tackle hard problems by working together.

As we get started, there will be several opportunities to join our group. Please stay tuned!  

***PhD applicants:*** Please apply through the [EMBL International PhD Programme.](https://www.embl.org/about/info/embl-international-phd-programme/) Applications are opened twice a year, once in summer and once in winter. Please see the program website for application instructions and relevant deadlines.
